# A Refresher

This second edition expands the original refresher in three ways.

First, the political-economy section now includes a Keynesian macroeconomic-management section, a substantially deeper treatment of the Varieties of Capitalism framework, and a refreshed survey of the post-2008 and post-COVID debates between Keynesian, monetarist, and Modern Monetary Theory positions.

Second, the American-government section adds a focused treatment of the 2024 administrative-law revolution — *Loper Bright*, *Corner Post*, *SEC v. Jarkesy*, and *Trump v. United States* — which collectively constitutes the most consequential structural shift in U.S. constitutional doctrine in forty years.

Third, throughout the document, contemporary peer-reviewed empirical findings have been integrated, replacing earlier reliance on conceptual claims alone.

The visual program is new: timelines, comparative matrices, and structural diagrams in LGR brand styling are interspersed where they earn their place. A reference table of major SCOTUS cases and a working glossary appear at the end.

As before: this is a reference, not a textbook. Read top-to-bottom, dip in by section, or pull a diagram in isolation as needed.
